# Research status

**Updated:** August 13, 2026

## Publication-level results

The universal-labeling draft proves the cyclic/all-abelian classification, the exact signed-sum support theorem and cancellation/range/parity criterion, Johnson set-system rigidity, several structural graph classifications, complementary-neighborhood obstructions, phantom periods, and the elementary-abelian-2 switching classification.

The finite-field draft proves the translation-sum/Hasse framework, affine-line filters, nonlinear constructions in dimension three, all admissible even dimensions, and every fixed odd dimension at least five for infinitely many primes, plus a dimension-two obstruction for the full-line architecture.

The order-nine theorem is backed by a complete certificate archive covering all 274,668 unlabeled simple graphs and all 205,914 eligible classes, with two independent verifiers. The heavy archive remains at https://github.com/zach7036/distance-antimagic-order9.

## Explicitly unresolved

- The Kamatchi-Arumugam conjecture is not solved in general.
- The fixed noncyclic-group pair problem is not fully classified for groups of exponent greater than two.
- The even-degree Cayley problem on elementary abelian 2-groups is not fully classified.
- Priority remains provisional pending specialist and database-level review.

Proofs carry theorem claims. Computation is used for certificates, regression tests, finite verification, and falsification—not as a hidden substitute for proof.
